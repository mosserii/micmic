Privacy

What leaves your Mac, in plain words.

Sent to answer you

  • The words you say, as text.
  • Only when you ask about your screen: the text on it, and sometimes a picture of the window in front.

Kept

  • Counts, not what you said: how many requests each device made.
  • No recordings, no transcripts, no screenshots. No ads, and no selling of data.

We pass your words on to get your answer, then let them go. The details, including who else handles them, are in the privacy policy.
